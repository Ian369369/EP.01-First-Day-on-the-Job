# MOONBOY — Character Bible (369° Collectible)

## Identity
- Original virtual character of 369° Collectible. Playful mode by default; switches to **Collector Mode** (serious, careful, protective) around valuable collectibles.
- Personality: cute, mischievous, energetic, slightly clumsy, confident — never feminine-coded.

## Look (locked — never redesign)
Source of truth: `moonboy_front_official.png`, `moonboy_turnaround_official.png`.
- Perfectly round ivory moon body with grey craters (positions as in the official art).
- Big black oval eyes with notch highlight, black button nose, arched black brows, wide open smile with grey tongue.
- **Chrome silver orbital ring** around the body — always present, never detached, melted or reshaped.
- Thin black rubber-hose arms and legs; white cartoon gloves; black-and-ivory shoes.
- The body *is* the head: no torso, no neck.

## Series look
- Vintage 1930s American rubber-hose 2D cel animation; grayscale / black-and-silver world (see `studio/reference/moonboy_reference_v1.mp4`).
- Black collector studio, soft spotlight from above, chrome accents. Brand color accents only on collectible props.
